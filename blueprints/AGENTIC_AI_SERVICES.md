# 🛍️ Productized Service Tiers: Agentic AI Architecture Blueprint

This document outlines the productized B2B AI consulting tiers designed for Upwork Project Catalog, Djinni, and direct client acquisition.

---

### 📌 Tier 1: Discovery Blueprint ($499)
* **Delivery Time:** 3 Business Days
* **Target Audience:** Startups or SMBs wanting to validate an AI agent concept before coding.
* **Deliverables:**
  1. **Agent Topology Document:** Selection of frameworks (LangChain / CrewAI / OpenAI Swarm) and model routing logic.
  2. **Tool Definition Schemas:** OpenAPI / JSON-RPC schemas for up to 3 agent tools.
  3. **RAG & Vector Strategy:** Guidance on vector database selection (pgvector / Qdrant / Pinecone) and chunking parameters.
  4. **1-Hour Technical Alignment Call:** Reviewing recommendations with the engineering lead.

---

### 📌 Tier 2: Enterprise Swarm Architecture ($799)
* **Delivery Time:** 5 Business Days
* **Target Audience:** Mid-market SaaS companies adding multi-agent systems to existing production platforms.
* **Deliverables:**
  1. **Multi-Agent Swarm Specification:** Planner + Adversarial Critic (Red Team) + Executor topology with state-passing schemas.
  2. **Model Context Protocol (MCP) Integration Spec:** Enterprise MCP server/client design for database and third-party CRM tools.
  3. **Human-In-The-Loop (HITL) Safety & Guardrails:** Rate-limiting rules, approval threshold gates ($500+ transfer protections), and prompt injection defenses.
  4. **Hybrid Search Architecture:** Dense + Sparse BM25 retrieval strategy with Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF).
  5. **Two 1-Hour Strategy Calls:** Discovery + Final Handoff Review.

---

### 📌 Tier 3: Full Platform Architecture & Eval Harness ($1,299)
* **Delivery Time:** 7 Business Days
* **Target Audience:** Enterprise platforms needing end-to-end production readiness, security compliance, and continuous LLM evaluation.
* **Deliverables:**
  1. **Complete Multi-Agent & RAG Architecture Package:** All Tier 1 & Tier 2 deliverables.
  2. **Eval-Driven Development (EDD) Suite:** LLM-as-a-Judge test harness specification, tool-calling precision metrics, and cost-per-task benchmark suite.
  3. **Token Economics & Metering Plan:** COGS calculator, caching strategies (Prompt Caching / Semantic Caching), and multi-tenant billing architecture.
  4. **Security & Regulatory Compliance Audit:** AST parameter scanning, audit logging schema, and RBAC data access policies.
  5. **3 Weeks of Async Slack / Email Support:** Ongoing guidance during engineering implementation.
